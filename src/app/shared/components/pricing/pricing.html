Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade my plan later?

expand_more
Absolutely! You can switch from a Student account to a Teacher account at any time from your profile settings. All your current course progress will be preserved.

What payment methods are accepted?

expand_more
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, Net Banking, and popular digital wallets. All transactions are secured with industry-standard encryption.

Are there any hidden fees?

expand_more
No. What you see is what you pay. Student courses are billed per enrollment, and teacher uploads are billed per course launched. There are no annual platform maintenance fees.
